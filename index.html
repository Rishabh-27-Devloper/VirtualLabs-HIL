VirtualLab-HIL — Mixed-Signal Circuit Simulator & ESP32 Hardware-in-the-Loop

VirtualLab-HIL is an interactive browser-based electronic circuit simulator for analog, digital, and ESP32 hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) mixed-signal circuits.

Key Features

  • Circuit Simulator: Interactive SPICE-accurate Modified Nodal Analysis with semiconductor physics.
  • ESP32 Hardware in the Loop: Real-time hardware telemetry stream over WebSockets.
  • VirtualLab Instruments: 2-channel digital oscilloscope, 4-channel logic analyzer, signal generator.
  • Digital Logic Solver: Multi-state logic gates, propagation delays, D-latches, and truth tables.
